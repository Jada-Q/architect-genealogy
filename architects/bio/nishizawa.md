## Bio
Ryue Nishizawa Japanese architect based in Tokyo (born 1966) [[Pritzker Prize]] (2010)<br>[[Royal Gold Medal|RIBA Royal Gold Medal]] (2025)"}},"i":0}}]}' id="mwBw"> Ryue Nishizawa ( 西沢 立衛 , Nishizawa Ryūe ; born 1966 in Kanagawa Prefecture ) is a Japanese architect based in Tokyo . A graduate of Yokohama National University , he established his own firm, Office of Ryue Nishizawa, in 1997. In 1995, he co-founded the firm SANAA (Sejima and Nishizawa and Associates) with architect Kazuyo Sejima . In 2010, he became the youngest recipient ever of the Pritzker Prize , together with Sejima.
