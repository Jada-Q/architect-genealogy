## Bio
### Career
Naito was born in 1950 in Yokohama , Japan. He received a M.Arch from Graduate School of Waseda University . He was chief architect at Fernand Higueras in Madrid, Spain, from 1976 to 1978, and worked at Kikutake Architects in Tokyo from 1979 to 1981. Naito established Naito Architect & Associates in 1981. Naito designed a "dog cooler" for Spitz .
